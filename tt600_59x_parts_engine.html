Engine Workflow Checklist

  • Change engine oil and filter before judging engine behavior.
  • Replace the spark plug as part of the baseline service.
  • Replace the drain washer whenever the oil service is done.
  • Decide whether to fit the magnetic drain plug during the first service cycle.
  • Refresh tired cover screws if the original JIS heads are damaged.
