## Order 4: 3TB donor electrics

This is the highest-risk purchase group. Do not scatter-buy unless the used market leaves no alternative.

### Preferred order pattern

1. One complete XT600 3TB electrical lot.
2. If no complete lot appears, buy stator + flywheel + CDI + regulator/rectifier only from sellers who can prove donor compatibility.
3. Keep ignition coil as a secondary piece if the main lot does not include it.

### Planning range for used donor path

| Part | Planning range |
|---|---|
| 3TB stator | EUR 60-140 |
| 3TB flywheel | EUR 40-100 |
| 3TB CDI | EUR 80-180 |
| 3TB regulator/rectifier | EUR 40-120 |
| 12V ignition coil | EUR 20-60 |

### Donor-order logic

- The correct financial unit is the system, not the individual part.
- A cheap unmatched CDI can become the most expensive mistake in the whole conversion.
- Prioritize complete-lot listings and donor bikes over isolated loose parts.

## Best purchase sequence overall

1. KEDO service basket.
2. OEM Yamaha small parts only if needed.
3. Loom materials and battery.
4. Matched 3TB donor electricals.
5. Only then complete the 12V build and commissioning.

## What to avoid

- Separate micro-orders for washers, bulbs, and connector consumables.
- Buying the CDI first just because it is available.
- Buying stator or flywheel without evidence they belong to the same electrical family.
- Delaying the intake rubber and carb service while focusing on the conversion.
