## D) What to actually purchase first

1. Buy-now fixed basket from section A.
2. Choose CDI path:
new aftermarket CDI now, or used 3TB donor CDI with full matched set.
3. Acquire stator + flywheel + regulator + CDI as one matched 3TB group.

## E) Seller checks for each used electrical part

- Ask donor model code and year.
- Ask if stator, flywheel, CDI, regulator came from the same running bike.
- Ask for clear connector and part-marking photos.
- Ask for stator/pickup resistance values and charging behavior before strip-down.
