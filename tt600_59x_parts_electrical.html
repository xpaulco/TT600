This page exists to stop the most expensive mistake in the project: buying fragmented or mismatched XT600 3TB electrical parts. Treat this page as the canonical conversion shopping list.

Open CDI decisionSeller templates

Primary rule

Stator, flywheel, CDI, and regulator/rectifier must be electrically compatible as one system. The best buy is a matched 3TB donor set, not isolated cheap parts.

Electrical & 12V Checklist

  • Confirm the project stays on the full XT600 3TB matched-system path.
  • Decide battery, regulator, and fuse block locations before making the loom.
  • Buy loom materials and all 12V bulbs together.
  • Hunt a complete 3TB donor set before buying isolated parts.
  • Bench-test spark before final loom wrapping.
  • Confirm charging voltage stays in the 13.8V to 14.5V range.
