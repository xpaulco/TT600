# Yamaha TT600 59X Compatibility Matrix

Scope: consolidate compatibility claims already present in this workspace for a TT600 59X restoration project. Treat this as a working reference, not a final parts fiche. Anything marked Verify should be confirmed against OEM part numbers or measurements before purchase.

## Model anchors

- TT600 59X: core project bike, around 1987-1988 in the existing notes.
- XT600 43F and 2KF: most frequently cited early interchange donors.
- XT600 3AJ and 1VJ: cited mainly for suspension or airbox family references.
- XT600 3TB: cited as the matched donor set for 12V electrical conversion, not as a mix-and-match source.

## What appears safe to search under XT600

- Speedometer drive gearbox
- Brake and clutch levers
- Carb rebuild kit parts
- Choke and speedometer cables
- Cam-chain tensioner listings mentioned in the mod guide
- Rear shock family references 43F, 1VJ, 3AJ

## What should be treated as 59X-specific until proven otherwise

- CDI advance curve and any ignition box substitution
- Original 6V electrical layout and capacitor
- Battery packaging constraints due to the frame oil tank
- Cylinder decisions involving the Nikasil bore
- Any centre-stand or frame-mounted accessory fitment

## Verify-before-buy list

- Oil filler / dipstick thread for the 59X oil tank
- Fork diameter and spring length before ordering XT600-family fork springs
- Exact CDI model code compatibility for aftermarket replacements
- Centre-stand bracket geometry on the 59X frame
- Any big-bore plan: confirm whether the engine still has the original Nikasil cylinder or already an XT steel-sleeve conversion
