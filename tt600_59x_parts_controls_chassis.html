Rideability and safety

Controls, brake, cable, chain, and speedo parts in one page.

These are the parts that turn a static restoration into a bike that can actually be ridden safely and assessed honestly.

What belongs here

Brake pads, hose, levers, cables, chain kit, master link, and speedometer-drive related parts.

Controls & Drive Checklist

  • Check brake pads, brake fluid, and caliper movement before road testing.
  • Replace worn chain and sprockets before judging the bike on the road.
  • Secure working levers and cables before regular riding.
  • Inspect the speedometer drive if the front wheel feels notchy.
