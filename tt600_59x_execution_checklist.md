# TT600 59X Execution Checklist

Date: 2026-09-04

Use this as the single-page action list for the restoration and the 12V conversion. Work top to bottom. Do not skip the dependency order.

## Phase 1: baseline mechanical truth

- [ ] Order and receive the KEDO service basket.
- [ ] Inspect intake manifolds for cracks and hardening.
- [ ] Rebuild the carburettor with fresh service parts.
- [ ] Replace air filter and spark plug.
- [ ] Change engine oil and filter.
- [ ] Replace drain washer and decide whether to fit the magnetic drain plug.
- [ ] Check fork condition, fork seals, and fork oil state.
- [ ] Check brake pads, brake fluid, and caliper piston movement.

## Phase 2: small OEM and rubber details

- [ ] Order the Yamaha tank silent-bloc if yours is worn or missing.
- [ ] Inspect all tank and body mounting rubbers before reassembly.

## Phase 3: choose and prepare the 12V architecture

- [ ] Confirm the project stays on the full XT600 3TB matched-system path.
- [ ] Read the CDI decision sheet before buying any ignition box.
- [ ] Decide battery location: right side panel bracket or rear subframe area.
- [ ] Decide regulator/rectifier location with airflow near the right side of the airbox.
- [ ] Decide fuse block location: side panel area or under headstock.

## Phase 4: buy loom materials

- [ ] Buy AGM battery in the YTX5L-BS class.
- [ ] Buy 4 to 6 way ATO/ATC fuse block.
- [ ] Buy weatherproof inline fuse holder.
- [ ] Buy 5A, 10A, and 20A fuse assortment.
- [ ] Buy TXL or GXL wire in the gauges required by the loom guide.
- [ ] Buy Superseal and/or Deutsch connector kits.
- [ ] Buy adhesive-lined heat shrink.
- [ ] Buy Tesa 51036 loom tape.
- [ ] Buy split conduit.
- [ ] Buy all 12V replacement bulbs together.

## Phase 5: source donor electrics

- [ ] Hunt a complete XT600 3TB donor electrical set first.
- [ ] If no full set exists, source stator and flywheel as a matched pair.
- [ ] Confirm CDI reference and connector style before purchase.
- [ ] Confirm regulator/rectifier reference 3TJ-81960-01 or an accepted equivalent.
- [ ] Confirm ignition coil is 12V and within the expected primary resistance range.
- [ ] Prefer sellers who can prove the parts came from one running bike.

## Phase 6: bench verification before loom finalization

- [ ] Lay out battery, CDI, regulator/rectifier, and fuse block on the bare frame first.
- [ ] Keep CDI trigger wire path separated from the HT lead by at least 50 mm.
- [ ] Build the minimum ignition test circuit first: stator trigger to CDI to coil to plug.
- [ ] Confirm spark before building the full loom.
- [ ] Build the charging side only after the spark path is confirmed.
- [ ] Use a single earth point for every return path.

## Phase 7: full loom build

- [ ] Wire main battery feed with 20A protection close to battery positive.
- [ ] Wire ignition/CDI/coil circuit on 5A fuse.
- [ ] Wire headlight on 10A fuse.
- [ ] Wire tail/brake light on 5A fuse.
- [ ] Wire instruments on separate 5A fuse.
- [ ] Add spare accessory circuit only after core circuits are clean.
- [ ] Protect all joints and entries with adhesive-lined heat shrink.
- [ ] Wrap the loom only after testing, not before.

## Phase 8: commissioning checks

- [ ] Measure charging voltage at about 3000 rpm.
- [ ] Confirm target charging range is 13.8V to 14.5V DC.
- [ ] Confirm all earth paths are under 0.2 ohm to battery negative.
- [ ] Confirm kill switch stops the engine by grounding the CDI correctly.
- [ ] Confirm all bulbs are 12V and survive first power-up.
- [ ] Confirm regulator/rectifier does not overheat in its mounted position.

## Phase 9: close-out

- [ ] Replace estimate prices in the budget sheet with real prices paid.
- [ ] Update donor leads with actual sellers and outcomes.
- [ ] Update the shopping map with final chosen listings.
- [ ] Draw or keep the final as-built wiring layout with the bike documents.

## Stop rules

- [ ] Stop if you are about to buy isolated 3TB parts with unclear provenance.
- [ ] Stop if a seller cannot show donor model, connector style, or part markings.
- [ ] Stop if charging exceeds 15V during testing.
- [ ] Stop if any 6V bulb remains in the bike at first 12V power-up.
