Safe XT600 search territory

  • Speedometer drive gearbox
  • Brake and clutch levers
  • Carb rebuild kit parts
  • Choke and speedometer cables
  • Rear shock family references 43F, 1VJ, 3AJ

Keep 59X caution

  • CDI advance curve and ignition substitution
  • Original 6V architecture and capacitor
  • Battery packaging around the frame oil tank
  • Nikasil cylinder decisions
  • Centre-stand and frame-mounted accessory fitment

Mandatory donor electronics

  • XT600 3TB stator
  • XT600 3TB flywheel rotor
  • Regulator/rectifier 3TJ-81960-01
  • CDI 3TB-82305-00 or 3TB-82305-10 family
  • 12V ignition coil
  • YTX5L-BS class AGM battery

Mandatory loom materials

  • 4 to 6 way ATO/ATC fuse block
  • Main inline fuse holder
  • 5A, 10A, 20A fuse assortment
  • TXL or GXL wire set
  • Superseal or Deutsch connectors
  • Heat shrink, loom tape, and split conduit

CDI Path Decision

Used matched donor first. Aftermarket fallback second.

Electrical parts

Path A: used 3TB donor CDI

  • Target references: 3TB-82305-00 or 3TB-82305-10 family.
  • Best when acquired inside a complete donor electrical set.
  • Planning range for CDI alone: €80-180.
  • Best-value path if provenance is strong.

Path B: aftermarket CDI

  • Current live price: $299.99.
  • Replaces 3TB-82305-00-00 and 3TB-82305-10-00.
  • Useful when donor CDI provenance is weak.
  • Still only sensible inside a full 3TB conversion.
Bottom line: the real engineering choice is matched donor system versus fragmented parts, not used CDI versus aftermarket CDI in isolation.
Execution Checklist

Do the restoration and 12V conversion in dependency order.

Parts catalog

Phase 1-3

  • Order KEDO service basket.
  • Refresh intake, carb, air filter, plug, oil, brakes, and fork basics.
  • Confirm full XT600 3TB matched-system path.
  • Lock battery, regulator, and fuse block locations.

Phase 4-6

  • Buy loom materials and all 12V bulbs together.
  • Hunt complete 3TB donor electrics first.
  • Bench test minimum ignition circuit before full loom build.
  • Keep CDI trigger path away from the HT lead.

Phase 7-9

  • Build fused circuits in the planned order.
  • Verify charging at 13.8V to 14.5V DC around 3000 rpm.
  • Verify earth continuity below 0.2 ohm.
  • Update budget and donor sheets with real outcomes.
Seller Templates

Short, full, and part-specific messages for 3TB donor parts.

Electrical parts Donor leads CSV

Short outreach

Use the short French or English message first to confirm donor model, year, visible part number, testing status, and connector photos.

Best first question: is it really from an XT600 3TB, and can the seller prove it with markings and connectors?

Fast rejection rules

  • Walk away if the seller cannot name the donor model.
  • Walk away if they cannot provide part-number photos.
  • Walk away if they claim many XT/TT fits but no source bike.
  • Walk away from isolated random parts with no provenance.
