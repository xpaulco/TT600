Carb & Intake Checklist

  • Inspect intake manifolds for cracks and hardening.
  • Rebuild the carburettor with fresh service parts before tuning anything else.
  • Replace the air filter together with the carb refresh.
  • Confirm cable condition if the choke control feels stiff or damaged.
