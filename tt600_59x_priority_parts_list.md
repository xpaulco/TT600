## Tier 5: electrical strategy fork in the road

Choose one path before buying random electrical parts.

### Path A: keep the original 6V layout

Buy only what supports a stable original system.

- Healthy capacitor
- Correct bulbs
- Repairs to original connectors and loom

### Path B: do the full 12V conversion

Do not buy isolated pieces. Buy the complete XT600 3TB matched donor set.

- Stator
- Flywheel
- CDI
- Regulator / rectifier
- 12V coil
- Battery and mounting plan
- Full bulb conversion

## Defer until the bike is mechanically sorted

- Decals
- Side plates
- Front fender
- Handguards unless the bike will be ridden off-road immediately
- Centre stand until frame fitment is confirmed
- Big-bore or performance engine upgrades

## First practical move

If you want the lowest-risk start, fill the tracker CSV first with the Tier 1 and Tier 2 items and note whether each one is:

- already on the bike and acceptable
- needs replacement
- needs verification by measurement or OEM number

That will separate restoration essentials from nice-to-have spending very quickly.
