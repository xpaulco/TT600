## What I would buy new right now

This is the low-risk basket you can order immediately without waiting for used donor parts.

1. KEDO oil filter kit 91628
2. KEDO air filter 33112
3. Engine cover Allen screw set 10236 if your JIS screws are tired
4. Standard service consumables already present in your workbook: oil, fork oil, brake fluid, drain washers, brake pads, spark plug
5. Intake manifolds once you confirm the ecomotospieces or KEDO fitment against your carb and head

## What I would not buy piecemeal

- a single 3TB CDI without the rest of the donor strategy decided
- random regulator / rectifier listings without proof of donor model
- isolated stator or flywheel parts when the seller cannot confirm the matching 3TB set
- used unknown electronics with no connector photos or resistance checks

## Best immediate buying plan

### Basket A: buy now, low risk

- oil filter kit
- air filter
- service consumables
- intake rubber parts

### Basket B: wait for the right used donor lot

- stator
- flywheel
- CDI
- regulator / rectifier
- coil

### Basket C: confirm before buying

- battery choice and mounting bracket plan
- 12V bulbs and lighting spec
- CDI mounting and connector adaptation

## Questions to send sellers of 3TB electrical parts

- What exact bike and model code did this come from?
- Are the stator, flywheel, CDI, and regulator from the same running bike?
- Can you send clear photos of all connector plugs and wire colors?
- Can you send resistance readings for the stator and pickup coil?
- Was the bike charging correctly before dismantling?
